I've put down some one liner thoughts for different scenarios, hope they can can serve as food for thought. I plan to keep updating them as I "grow" and tag them with relevant art(paintings, speeches, talks, interviews, ...) :P

**Legacy**

Things built over time can have good lessons to offer. Example: Growth vs preservation

<a href="https://youtu.be/VKiDPSuCIuQ?t=89">Bo Burnham about long term work</a><br>

**Toolkit**

When solving problems make sure you have proper toolkit.

**Judgement**

Considerate judgement needs investment.

**Spectrum**

Qualities(Example: Trustworthiness, introversion, ..) can be a spectrum instead of being just 1/0.

**Confidence**

Confidence can be situational.

<a href="https://youtu.be/UM7iL0HCcM0?t=60">Ringo Starr talks about being nervous before performing</a><br>


**Validation**

It can be helpful to check if validation is being sought at the right place.

**Change**

Change can be scary, it can help to understand the impact.

**Anxiety**

Anxiety comes from feeling out of control. While it cannot be completely eliminated, being aware of where it's coming by asking ourselves why, can make us feel slightly better. Anxiety can help us explore new possibilities and make us grow as a person.

**Problems**

Seemingly small problems can be big, given the situation.

**Emotional downtimes**

Downtime may not be as bad when you zoom out of the situation.

**Popularity**

Popularity may not always imply resourcefulness. It's important to understand the purpose that's being served.

**Control**

It can help to understand how much control we have over situations we are dwelling upon.

**Simplicity**

Seemingly simple, functioning systems can be harder to build and easier to operate.

**Effort**

Putting in conscious effort into something almost never goes wasted.

**Criticism**

Negative criticism, although hurts at the instant can also be used as a fuel.

**Haters**

It's important to realise not everyone has your best interests but being reminded of purpose you've set for yourself in the past can help.

**Dreams**

Use them to keep you moving!

**Exceptions**

When analyzing a scenario making sure it's not an exception/one-off can be helpful.

**Time**

Time can answer a few questions.

**Evolution**

Systems evolve with time. Almost none started as the same version.

**Fitting in**

Fitting in can be easier if a common ground can be established.

**Loneliness**

We often think we are the only one experiencing a certain scenario which makes us more lonely.

**Groups**

Common goal can tie groups together.

**Actions**

Actions can pretty much always have more impact than words.

**Accuracy**

Sometimes, going an extra mile for more accuracy may not be needed.

**Experience**

Diverse experiences can lead to better outcomes.

**Communication**

Most communication can be non verbal.

**Oppression games**

It doesn't always have to be a zero sum game.

**Result**

Journey has a lot more to offer than the end result.

**Quality**

It can help to also understand quality while analyzing for quantity.

**Understanding**

Being aware of how much we don't understand a concept/situation can humble us while learning.

**Influence**

It's important to be aware of influence that's being laid upon.
