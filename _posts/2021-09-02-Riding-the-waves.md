I've put down some one liner thoughts for different scenarios, hope they can can serve as food for thought. I plan to keep updating them as I "grow" :P

**Legacy**

Things built over time can have good lessons to offer.

**Judgement**

Considerate judgement needs investment.

**Spectrum**

Qualities(Example: Trustworthiness, introversion, ..) can be a spectrum instead of being just 1/0.

**Confidence**

Confidence can be situational.

**Validation**

It can be helpful to check if validation is being sought at the right place.

**Change**

Change can be scary, it can help to understand the impact.

**Anxiety**

Anxiety comes from feeling out of control. While it cannot be completely eliminated, being aware of where it's coming from, can make us feel slightly better.

**Problems**

Seemingly small problems can be big, given the situation.

**Popularity**

Popularity may not always imply resourcefulness. It's important to understand the purpose that's being served.

**Control**

It can help to understand how much control we have over situations we are dwelling upon.

**Simplicity**

Seemingly simple, functioning systems can be harder to build and easier to operate.

**Time**

Time can answer a few questions.

**Evolution**

Systems evolve with time. Almost none started as the same version.

**Fitting in**

Fitting in can be easier if a common ground can be established.

**Actions**

Actions can pretty much always have more impact than words.

**Accuracy**

Sometimes, going an extra mile for more accuracy may not be needed.

**Experience**

Diverse experiences can lead to better outcomes.

**Communication**

Most communication can be non verbal.

**Oppression games**

It doesn't always have to be a zero sum game.

**Result**

Journey has a lot more to offer than the end result.

**Quality**

It can help to also understand quality while analyzing for quantity.

**Understanding**

Being aware of how much we don't understand a concept/situation can humble us while learning.

**Influence**

It's important to be aware of influence that's being laid upon.
