Hello there!

'Record and Reflect on Audio Memories' is a fully private application to record and reflect on audio memories.
You can tag your memories with activities(for example: Road trip, Exciting emotion, Sad emotion, Dinner, …) and people you’re with.
It offers powerful filtering and insights on memories.

Think you can filter out and shuffle listen through all your memories while you were on road trips with specific friends
and also get insights on those memories.
