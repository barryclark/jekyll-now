<p style="text-indent: 20px; text-align: justify;">Applied mathematician whose research focuses on dynamical systems, computational neuroscience, systems biology, and inverse problems, with particular interest in reconstructing dynamical systems from noisy biological observations.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 20px; text-align: justify;">I am a Professor and Researcher in the <a href="https://www.matematica.uns.edu.ar/default.php">Department of Mathematics</a> at the <a href="https://uns.edu.ar">Universidad Nacional del Sur</a>. My work combines mathematical modeling, stochastic dynamics, and computational methods to investigate biological and neural systems.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 20px; text-align: justify;">My current research is primarily driven by understanding what information about biological systems can be recovered from incomplete or noisy observations.</p>
